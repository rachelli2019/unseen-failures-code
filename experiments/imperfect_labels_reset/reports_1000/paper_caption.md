Imperfect failure labeling with Q=P. Left: mean missing-mass upper bounds for perfect labels, PPI, and B-PAC with fixed zeta=0.05 and delta=0.025. Perfect/PPI trajectories do not depend on the stopping threshold and are shown once. Shading shows pointwise 2.5th–97.5th percentiles across trials. Center: empirical simultaneous coverage through each sample count; the nominal level is 0.95. B-PAC uses the same fixed zeta=0.05 and delta=0.025 as in the upper-bound panel. Right: mean cumulative ground-truth label calls through stopping, including all forced B-PAC queries. Error bars show 2.5th–97.5th percentiles across trials. These ranges describe trial variability, not confidence intervals for the mean or simultaneous bands. Only thresholds where all 1,000 trials stopped in every method are shown. The 273 PPI trials unfinished at 10,000 draws were continued to stopping (last stop at 14,858 draws); costs include their full trajectories, not just the original horizon or the initially completed subset. In the cost sweep B-PAC uses zeta=epsilon/2 and delta=0.025. Categorizer calls are reported separately in the results table. Results use 1,000 paired trials with a 10,000-session coverage horizon.
